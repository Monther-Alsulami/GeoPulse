## Main sections

### 1\. Structural Geology

This is the main educational section.

#### Introduction to Structural Geology

* What structural geology studies and why it matters.
* Descriptive approach: observing and describing structures.
* Kinematic approach: interpreting deformation and movement.
* Dynamic approach: interpreting forces and stresses.
* Stress and strain fundamentals.
* Stereonets and projection were included in the earlier content plan; confirm their place in the final lesson sequence.

#### Types of Deformation

* Brittle deformation: fractures, joints, faults, and fault kinematics.
* Ductile deformation: folds and associated structures.
* Explain concepts with clear figures and examples appropriate for geology students.
